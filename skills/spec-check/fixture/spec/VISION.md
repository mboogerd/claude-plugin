# pantry: vision

For households who shop together, pantry is one shared shopping list that lives on their own
phones, unlike shopping apps that keep the list on a company's server.

## Why

Households lose track of what is needed because each person keeps a separate list, and the shared
apps that exist hold the list, and what it says about the household, on someone else's server.

## Goals

- **V1 One list.** Everyone in a household sees the same list within a minute of a change.
- **V2 Own data.** The list stays on the household's own phones.
- **V3 Quick add.** A person adds an item in a few seconds, with one hand.

## Non-goals

- **VB1** pantry has no accounts and no server that holds a list.
- **VB2** pantry never suggests products and never shows advertising.

## Success

A household of four uses one list for a month and nobody keeps a second one.
